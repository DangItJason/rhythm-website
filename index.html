How care starts

The nurse comes first. The paperwork comes after.

Here is the whole sequence, in order. No surprises between the first call and the first shift.

The call

Call or text anytime. A person answers, day or night. We talk through what is happening and what your family needs, and we schedule the assessment visit. No scripts, no pressure.

The RN visit

Our per-diem RN comes to the house, meets your mom or dad, and does the free assessment: their routine, what help is wanted, and a walk-through home safety review. Nothing is signed at this visit.

The first shift

If we accept the case, the agreement arrives by DocuSign to read and e-sign at home. We confirm your caregiver and a named backup, and then care begins.

Most agencies do this backwards. A signature and a deposit come first, and a nurse meets your parent weeks later, if at all. We think you should meet the nurse and see the plan before you are asked to commit to anything. So that is the order we run.

What $52 does not hide

No deposit. Some agencies hold two weeks of care, $800 or more, against your final invoice. We hold nothing.

No short-visit penalty. The four-hour visit costs the same hourly rate as the eight-hour one. No tier ladder.

No fee stack. The RN assessment is free. There is no separate nursing fee, supply surcharge, or weekend premium.

Holidays, plainly. $78/hour, 1.5 times the standard rate, on six designated holidays. That is the whole rate card.

Rides at cost. A caregiver's personal-vehicle miles on your errand are billed at $0.76 per mile with no markup. Tolls and parking at actual cost, with receipts.

Cancel with 24 hours' notice. Inside that window, the scheduled visit is billed. Emergencies are waived.

In writing before you sign. A proposed schedule and weekly estimate arrive with the agreement, so the math is on paper before you commit.

Long-term care insurance and Massachusetts veterans' programs may cover part of the cost. We will help you check before you commit to anything.

Real questions

Questions we hear at 11pm

How fast can care actually start?

There is no fixed clock, and we would rather be straight with you than promise one. The sequence is: a call, the free RN assessment, the e-signed agreement, confirmed caregiver and backup coverage, then the first shift. Coming home from a hospital or rehab? Say so when you call and we will move as fast as we safely can.

Will my mom have the same caregiver every visit?

That is the plan we confirm before care starts: one primary caregiver on set days, plus a familiar backup who has already met your parent, so a sick day does not mean a stranger at the door.

What happens if a caregiver cannot make a shift?

Every case has a named backup confirmed before the first shift. If the schedule changes, you hear it from us first, with the plan, not after the fact.

Is this medical care?

No. Rhythm is a non-medical home care agency: daily living help, meals, rides, companionship, and medication reminders. A registered nurse assesses every client and writes the care plan. If your parent needs skilled nursing, therapy, or wound care, we will say so and help you find the right provider.

Is there a contract or a deposit?

A service agreement, yes, with the rate and terms in writing. You e-sign it at home, after the RN assessment, never at the first meeting. A deposit, no. You pay for the hours you book.

My dad says he doesn't want a stranger in the house.

Most families start there. It is why the RN visit comes before any commitment, why caregivers are matched by personality, and why the plan is one primary caregiver on a set schedule. Strangers don't stay strangers on a schedule.
